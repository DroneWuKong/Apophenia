# Code of conduct

Apophenia should be a useful, careful, and welcoming project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing ideas, code, health experiences, or unusual observations.
- Critique claims and implementations without attacking people.
- Treat personal observations as sensitive information.
- Respect consent, privacy, and the evidence limits documented by the project.
- Help newcomers reproduce problems without shaming their device, skill level, or interpretation.
- Correct mistakes openly and distinguish uncertainty from fact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or targeted intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring someone to disclose medical, mental-health, location, or wearable data.
- Using the project to validate a diagnosis or to assert harmful medical, psychological, paranormal, or causal conclusions.
- Deliberately misrepresenting simulator results as physical-hardware evidence.

## Enforcement

For public project conduct, open a maintainer-contact issue only when doing so will not expose sensitive information. For private or sensitive reports, use GitHub's private security-advisory channel or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the safety or usefulness of the community. Reports will be reviewed in context and handled as privately as the available channel permits.
